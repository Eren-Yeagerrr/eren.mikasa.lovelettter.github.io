My Dear Sneha,

Even with the miles stretching between us, you are closer to me than my own heartbeat. Because we do not get to meet often, I carry our memories in every breath I take, holding onto them as the quiet proof that something truly beautiful exists in this world.

I love you more than I have ever loved myself. Every dream I have for tomorrow only makes sense because you are standing beside me in it. When I try to picture a world without your voice, your warmth, and your touch, everything simply fades to nothing. Losing you feels like losing the very air that keeps me alive.

Please do not let the distance make us forget what we have built. My future has always been written in your name.

Forever yours,Taaseen
